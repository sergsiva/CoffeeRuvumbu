Specialty coffee from the highlands of Rwanda. Farm-direct wholesale and curated farm tours for roasters, buyers, and coffee enthusiasts.

From our hills to your cup

Ruvumbu sits on the ridges of Nyamasheke, where altitude, volcanic soil and slow ripening produce exceptional Arabica.

We manage the full journey from seedling to export, which keeps traceability intact and lets us pay our partner farmers on terms they can plan around.

Every lot is hand-picked, processed with precision, and cupped at the station before it leaves for the port. Nothing ships that we have not tasted ourselves.

How a cherry becomes a shipment

Cultivation

Bourbon varietals grown at 1,700 m and above, shaded by banana trees.

Harvesting

Hand-picked at peak ripeness, then sorted by density and by defect.

Processing

Washed, natural or honey. Fermented, then dried on raised African beds.

Export

Cupped, graded and prepared for shipment straight to your roastery.
